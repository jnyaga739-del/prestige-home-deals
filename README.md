# Prestige Home Deals

Prestige Home Deals is an online home and kitchen appliances store serving customers across Kenya.

## What customers can do

- Browse home and kitchen appliances
- View product prices and descriptions
- Search products by category
- Order directly through WhatsApp

## How to Order

1. Choose a product.
2. Tap **Order on WhatsApp**.
3. Send your name, phone number, and delivery location.
4. We confirm availability, delivery cost, and the total amount before payment.
